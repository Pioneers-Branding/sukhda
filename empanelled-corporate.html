How Cashless Hospitalization Works at Sukhda

Our dedicated on-site TPA coordinators manage all approvals, document exchanges, and queries with your insurance provider so you can focus entirely on patient recovery.

01

TPA Registration

Present your health insurance card / TPA e-card along with patient government ID at our 24/7 TPA Desk in the main reception on admission.

02

Pre-Auth Request

Our insurance team fills out the cashless pre-authorization form with the consultant's clinical diagnosis and dispatches it within 30 minutes.

03

Initial Sanction

The insurer or TPA issues an initial approval letter, allowing cashless treatment, bed admission, diagnostics, and surgical intervention.

04

Final Settlement

Upon discharge advice, the final hospital bill and clinical summary are uploaded for final cashless settlement, enabling a hassle-free departure.

Patient Checklist

Documents Required for Cashless Claims

Please keep these original documents and photocopies ready at the time of planned or emergency admission:

Health Insurance Card / TPA E-Card with active Policy Number
Government Photo ID Proof of Patient & Primary Insured (Aadhaar / Voter ID / PAN)
Treating Doctor's Consultation Prescription & Admission Advice
Recent Diagnostic Investigation Reports (Blood, X-Ray, CT Scan, Ultrasound)
Employee ID Card / Dependent Card (for Corporate & PSU Beneficiaries)
For planned surgeries, bringing pre-authorization 48 hours in advance ensures zero waiting time on admission day.
