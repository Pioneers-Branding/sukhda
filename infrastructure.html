VISITING & FACILITY GUIDELINES

Frequently Asked Questions About Hospital Facilities

Helpful information regarding ICU visiting policies, attendant stay, and emergency admission.

What are the visiting hours for the Intensive Care Units (ICU) and general wards?
To maintain a quiet, healing environment and adhere to strict infection control, ICU visiting hours are restricted to 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM daily (one attendant at a time with shoe covers and hand sanitization). Inpatient wards allow visiting between 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM.
Can attendants stay overnight with the patient?
Yes, for Deluxe Suites, Private AC, and Semi-Private rooms, one designated attendant is permitted to stay overnight with the patient. Dedicated attendant couches/sofa-beds are provided in every private and semi-private room.
Does Sukhda Hospital provide emergency ambulance pickup in Hisar?
Yes, our 24*7 Advanced Life Support (ALS) and Basic Life Support (BLS) ambulances are stationed round-the-clock for prompt pickup across Hisar and adjoining regions of Haryana, Punjab, and Rajasthan. Call our emergency helpline at 01662-249473 for immediate dispatch.
Are cashless insurance facilities available for all room categories?
Yes, cashless hospitalisation is available across all room categories according to your insurance policy eligibility and TPA approval. Our 24*7 Cashless TPA Helpdesk assists patients with pre-authorization and smooth claim settlements.
What dining and food facilities are available for patients and attendants?
Inpatient meals are prepared fresh in our in-house dietary kitchen under the supervision of clinical dieticians according to each patient's medical condition. For attendants and visitors, a hygienic cafeteria operates on the campus serving fresh, wholesome food and refreshments.
