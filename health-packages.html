Important Guidelines

Preparing for Your Health Checkup

Following these simple instructions ensures accurate test values and a smooth morning appointment.

01

Overnight Fasting

Maintain 10 to 12 hours of overnight fasting before sample collection. Plain water is permitted.

02

Morning Medication

Take regular blood pressure medications with a sip of water. Diabetic medicines should be taken after the fasting blood draw.

03

Ultrasound Preparation

For abdominal ultrasound, reach on an empty stomach. For pelvic ultrasound, drink 3-4 glasses of water for a full bladder.

04

Previous Medical Records

Please bring previous discharge summaries, investigation reports, and current prescription slips for physician comparison.
