Cath Lab Advanced Diagnostic & Interventional Equipment

IMAGING INSIDE ARTERIES

IVUS Machine (Intravascular Ultrasound)

A miniature ultrasound probe on the catheter tip creates real-time, high-resolution 360° cross-sectional images of the coronary arteries.

  • Measures vessel diameter and plaque architecture accurately.
  • Identifies severity, length, and calcification of blockages.
  • Ensures optimal stent sizing and full wall apposition.
  • Substantially decreases post-PCI restenosis and stent thrombosis.
PHYSIOLOGICAL FLOW ASSESSMENT

FFR Machine (Fractional Flow Reserve)

A precision pressure-guidewire system that assesses whether an arterial stenosis is causing a significant reduction in blood flow to the heart muscle.

  • Measures exact pre- and post-lesion trans-stenotic pressure gradients.
  • Determines whether stent placement is required or medical therapy suffices.
  • Prevents unnecessary stent implantations in borderline lesions.
  • Essential for multi-vessel CAD strategic decision-making.
ANTICOAGULATION SAFETY

ACT Machine (Activated Clotting Time)

Point-of-care rapid testing measuring real-time clotting response to heparin anticoagulation during cardiac catheterisation.

  • Results within minutes, inside the sterile field.
  • Prevents thrombus formation inside catheter lines and stents.
  • Guards against bleeding complications due to over-anticoagulation.
MECHANICAL CIRCULATORY SUPPORT

IABP Machine (Intra-Aortic Balloon Pump)

A mechanical circulatory assist device synchronised with the cardiac rhythm to support failing hearts during acute cardiogenic shock or high-risk PCI.

  • Increases coronary perfusion pressure and myocardial oxygen delivery.
  • Significantly reduces left ventricular workload and afterload.
  • Vital bridge therapy for critical cardiac recovery and post-op care.
REAL-TIME CARDIAC ULTRASOUND

ECHO Machine (Echocardiography)

High-frequency Doppler echocardiography evaluating chamber dimensions, valve integrity, and left ventricular ejection fraction (EF).

  • Accurate quantification of systolic and diastolic cardiac function.
  • Detects pericardial effusion, valvular stenosis, regurgitation, and congenital defects.
  • Guides pericardiocentesis and transcatheter interventions.
24–48 HOUR RHYTHM TRACKING

Holter ECG & Advanced Defibrillators

Continuous portable ambulatory ECG monitoring paired with biphasic defibrillators and multi-parameter vital-sign telemetry.

  • Captures intermittent arrhythmias, silent ischaemia, syncope, and palpitations.
  • Synchronised cardioversion and external pacing for acute arrhythmias.
  • Evaluates pacemaker function and anti-arrhythmic drug efficacy.
RENAL CARE & NEPHROLOGY

Advanced Dialysis Unit

Round-the-clock specialised renal replacement therapy supervised by experienced nephrologists and certified dialysis technicians.

Dialysis Department Facilities

  • Advanced Haemodialysis with the latest-generation dialysis machines, ultra-pure water treatment and bicarbonate dialysate.
  • Direct supervision by Full-Time Consultant Nephrologist.
  • Peritoneal Dialysis (CAPD/APD) for acute and chronic renal failure patients.
  • USG-Guided Kidney Biopsy for native and transplanted kidneys.
  • ICU Nephrology Support for complex electrolyte, severe acid-base, and fluid imbalance disorders.
  • SLED (Sustained Low-Efficiency Dialysis) tailored for haemodynamically unstable and hypotensive patients.
  • 24/7 Emergency Dialysis availability with rapid catheter access.
