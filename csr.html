Sukhda Healthcare partnered with the USAID SAMRIDH Healthcare Blended Finance Facility and Pahel to strengthen primary and secondary healthcare infrastructure across 8 underserved rural blocks in Western Haryana. Through mobile diagnostic units, village-level telemedicine screening hubs, and maternal-child health follow-ups, we have brought super-specialist consultations directly to rural doorsteps.

Core Pillars of Action

Our Community Initiatives

Targeted healthcare programmes designed to bridge the urban-rural healthcare divide, promote early detection, and provide safety nets for underprivileged families.

Free Rural Multi-Speciality Camps

Regular on-ground medical camps in remote villages across Hisar, Hansi, Narnaund, and Barwala. Senior physicians, gynaecologists, and paediatricians provide free consultations, ECGs, blood glucose tests, and free prescription medicines.

120+ Village Camps Completed Annually

Community Cancer Screening Clinics

In association with Sukhda MedPark oncology specialists, we conduct free oral, breast, and cervical cancer awareness and screening clinics to identify malignancies at Stage 0/1 when cure rates exceed 90%.

12,000+ Screenings Conducted

Subsidized & BPL Dialysis Support

Ensuring no chronic kidney disease (CKD) patient goes without life-saving haemodialysis due to poverty. We provide highly subsidized and trust-funded dialysis slots using world-class Fresenius RO systems.

5,000+ Subsidized Sessions Delivered

Ayushman PM-JAY & Chirayu Facilitation

Dedicated on-campus helpdesks and village facilitation camps to assist eligible BPL families in creating Ayushman Golden Cards and receiving 100% cashless treatment across medical and surgical super-specialities.

100% Cashless Patient Navigation

Voluntary Blood Donation Drives

Quarterly mega voluntary blood donation camps organized in partnership with Red Cross Society, Rotary Club, and educational institutions, maintaining an active emergency rare-blood donor registry.

2,500+ Units Donated Annually

School Health, Nutrition & CPR Training

Adolescent anaemia screening drives for schoolgirls, nutritional counselling in rural schools, and hands-on CPR / Basic Life Support (BLS) training for college students, police, and auto-drivers.

15,000+ Students & Youths Trained
Real World Impact

Stories from the Field

How our community healthcare drives are making a tangible difference in rural Haryana.

Village Rawalwas Khurd, Hisar

Early Cervical Cancer Detection in 45-Year-Old Homemaker

During a Sukhda MedPark rural screening camp, Smt. Kamlesh was diagnosed with early-stage cervical dysplasia. She was promptly treated at MedPark with full financial assistance and is now healthy.

Village Sisai, Hansi Block

180+ Farmers Screened for Silent Hypertension & Diabetes

Our mobile medical van conducted point-of-care HbA1c and ECG checks, identifying 42 previously undiagnosed hypertensive patients who were provided free essential medications and dietary counselling.

Government Senior Secondary School, Hisar

Hands-Only CPR Training for 350 College Students

Sukhda emergency medicine faculty conducted practical mannequin simulation sessions, training young citizens as first responders in out-of-hospital cardiac arrest emergencies.
