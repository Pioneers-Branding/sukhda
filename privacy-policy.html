Section 1 Overview & Applicability

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, and transfer of personal data, medical information, and electronic records gathered by Sukhda Healthcare (comprising Sukhda Multispeciality Hospital, Delhi Road, Hisar, and Sukhda MedPark Cancer & Super-Speciality Hospital, NH 1P-4P, Sector-5, Hisar, Haryana), collectively referred to as "Sukhda", "the Hospital", "we", "us", or "our".

This policy applies to all patients, visitors, family members, healthcare practitioners, job applicants, and digital users who access our website (sukhdahealthcare.com), submit enquiry or appointment forms, interact with our helpline channels, or receive inpatient/outpatient clinical care.

Regulatory Compliance Framework: This policy is formulated under the provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the National Medical Commission Registered Medical Practitioner (Professional Conduct) Regulations, 2023.

Section 2 Personal & Healthcare Data We Collect

Depending on your interaction with Sukhda Healthcare, we may collect the following categories of personal and sensitive healthcare data:

  • Personal Contact & Identifiers: Full name, age, date of birth, gender, telephone number, mobile/WhatsApp number, email address, residential address, emergency contact person, and government identification (such as Aadhaar, ABHA ID, or Ayushman Card number for cashless navigation).
  • Clinical & Health Information: Consultation requests, clinical complaints, symptoms, previous medical history, diagnostic investigations, laboratory reports, discharge summaries, prescription records, and doctors' notes voluntarily submitted through online appointment portals, tele-consultation queries, or at the hospital reception desk.
  • Financial & Insurance Information: Empanelled TPA / health insurance policy details, corporate ID, cashless claim authorisation documents, and billing history strictly for payment settlement and insurance claims processing.
  • Technical & Session Data: Device IP address, browser type, operating system, website access timestamps, referring URLs, and language preferences (English/Hindi) stored via secure session cookies for seamless navigation.
  • Employment & Volunteer Data: Professional curriculum vitae (CV), educational qualifications, medical council registration certificates, and references submitted through our careers portal.

Section 3 Purpose of Data Collection & Healthcare Operations

We process personal and health data strictly for lawful, clinical, and administrative purposes, including:

  • Appointment Scheduling & OPD Coordination: Scheduling outpatient visits, routing consultation requests to relevant doctors, and issuing appointment reminders via call, SMS, or WhatsApp.
  • Clinical Diagnosis & Healthcare Delivery: Enabling physicians, surgeons, nurses, and laboratory specialists to evaluate patient history, plan treatment protocols, and deliver inpatient, outpatient, and emergency trauma care.
  • Emergency & Critical Care Assistance: Providing swift 24/7 emergency response, ICU bed allocation, and ambulance dispatch.
  • TPA & Cashless Claim Navigation: Coordinating pre-authorisations and insurance claims with government schemes (Ayushman Bharat, CGHS, ECHS, Haryana Govt.) and private TPAs.
  • Statutory & Quality Compliance: Maintaining electronic medical records in compliance with NMC regulations, NABH quality indicators, and legal reporting obligations for infectious diseases or medico-legal cases.
Zero Commercial Sale Guarantee: Sukhda Healthcare strictly prohibits and does not engage in the sale, rental, barter, or commercial marketing brokerage of your personal or medical data to third-party advertisers or telemarketers.

Section 4 Lawful Basis & Explicit Consent Mechanism

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, personal data is processed on the following lawful grounds:

  • Explicit Consent: Provided by you or your authorised representative when submitting online enquiry forms, appointment booking requests, or physical hospital admission registration forms.
  • Legitimate Medical Use & Medical Emergency: Processing essential patient data during life-threatening medical emergencies, trauma care, or infectious-disease reporting, where immediate consent cannot be obtained.
  • Statutory Obligation: Adhering to clinical record maintenance and public health directives issued by competent health authorities.

You have the right to withdraw your consent for non-essential communications (such as hospital health newsletters) at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the legality of processing completed before withdrawal, or the statutory preservation of clinical records.

Section 5 Data Storage, Security Protocols & Encryption

We implement stringent technical, administrative, and physical security measures to safeguard personal health data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or breach:

  • Encryption Standards: Transmission of sensitive form data over our website is secured using industry-standard SSL/TLS (HTTPS) encryption.
  • Role-Based Access Controls: Hospital Information Systems (HIS) and patient records are accessible only to authorised clinical practitioners, treating nurses, and designated administrative personnel on a need-to-know basis.
  • Secure Infrastructure: Physical servers, cloud backups, and network firewalls are continuously monitored with routine vulnerability assessments and audit trails.
  • Employee Confidentiality: All hospital staff, doctors, and allied health professionals are bound by strict non-disclosure agreements and ethical confidentiality obligations.

Section 6 Disclosure & Sharing of Information

Your personal and medical information is kept strictly confidential and shared only under the following limited conditions:

  • Treating Medical Team: Specialist doctors, duty physicians, anaesthetists, and intensive care nurses involved in your clinical treatment.
  • Diagnostic & Pharmacy Partners: In-house pathology labs, radiology departments, and hospital pharmacies to process prescriptions and investigation reports.
  • Empanelled TPAs & Health Insurers: Shared strictly upon patient request and authorisation to facilitate cashless hospitalisation or reimbursement claims.
  • Statutory & Legal Authorities: Shared when required by law, court summons, medico-legal requirements, or disease-surveillance authorities.

Section 7 Patient & User Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you are entitled to the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

Request a summary of your personal data processed by Sukhda Healthcare and the categories of healthcare entities it was shared with.

Right to Correction

Request correction, completion, or updating of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete personal contact and demographic details.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of non-essential personal data, subject to mandatory medical record retention laws governing hospitals.

Right to Withdraw

Withdraw consent for non-clinical communications, health awareness reminders, or promotional follow-ups at any time.

Right to Nominate

Nominate another individual to exercise your rights on your behalf in the event of incapacity or medical emergency.

Right to Grievance

Access transparent, time-bound grievance redressal through our designated Hospital Data Protection Officer.

Section 8 Medical Record Retention Protocols

Medical and clinical records (including inpatient case sheets, surgical notes, diagnostic imaging, and discharge summaries) are preserved in strict compliance with the statutory retention schedules mandated by the National Medical Commission (NMC), the Clinical Establishments Act, and NABH guidelines:

  • Outpatient (OPD) Records: Retained for a minimum of 3 years from the date of the last consultation.
  • Inpatient (IPD) Records: Retained for a minimum of 5 to 7 years from the date of discharge.
  • Medico-Legal Cases (MLC) & Paediatric Records: Preserved until the resolution of legal proceedings or until a paediatric patient reaches the age of majority plus the statutory limitation period.

Section 9 Cookies & Language Preference Notice

Our website uses functional cookies that are strictly necessary for the website's operation:

  • Language Preference Cookies (googtrans): Preserve your selected website language (English or Hindi) across all pages for seamless readability.
  • Session Cookies: Enable essential navigation, mobile menu interactions, and appointment form validations.

We do not employ third-party advertising cookies, behavioural profiling trackers, or cross-site tracking pixels that monitor your personal browsing habits across the internet.
