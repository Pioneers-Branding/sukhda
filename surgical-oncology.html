FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About Surgical Oncology (Modular OTs)

Clear, evidence-based answers to help you make informed decisions about your health and treatment options.

Ask a Doctor
What is Breast Conserving Surgery (BCS) and Oncoplasty?
Breast Conserving Surgery involves removing only the cancer lump with a rim of healthy tissue instead of the entire breast. Oncoplastic techniques rearrange surrounding breast tissue to maintain natural breast symmetry and appearance, followed by radiation therapy to ensure cure rates equal to full mastectomy.
What is an Intra-Operative Frozen Section Biopsy?
During surgery, a tissue sample from the margins of the removed tumor is immediately frozen and examined by our pathologist within 20 minutes. This confirms whether all cancer cells have been removed before the surgeon closes the incision, preventing second surgeries.
What is Microvascular Reconstruction in Head & Neck Cancer?
When a portion of the jaw, tongue, or mouth is removed during cancer surgery, tissue (bone, muscle, skin) is transplanted from another part of the body (e.g. leg fibula or arm) and its micro-blood vessels are reconnected under a microscope to restore speech, chewing, and facial appearance.
Why choose a dedicated Surgical Oncologist over a general surgeon for cancer?
Surgical Oncologists complete specialized super-specialty training (M.Ch) exclusively in cancer biology, radical lymph node dissections, organ-sparing techniques, and reconstruction, which significantly improves long-term cancer survival and lowers recurrence rates.
Are cancer surgeries covered under cashless insurance?
Yes. Sukhda MedPark provides cashless hospitalization for all major surgical oncology procedures under Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY), CGHS, ECHS, Haryana Govt health schemes, and all major insurance TPAs.
