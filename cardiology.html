OUR CARDIAC SERVICES

Specialised Diagnostic & Interventional Procedures

State-of-the-art cardiovascular interventions designed for rapid recovery and lasting heart health.

Coronary Angiography (CAG)

Painless day-care coronary angiography performed primarily through the radial (wrist) artery for minimal discomfort, zero bed rest, and same-day discharge.

Emergency Primary Angioplasty (PAMI)

Round-the-clock emergency ballooning and drug-eluting stent (DES) placement to restore blood flow to the heart muscle within the critical golden hour.

Pacemaker & Device Implantation

Permanent pacemaker implantation (PPI), Implantable Cardioverter Defibrillators (ICD), and CRT devices for bradycardia, heart block, and heart failure.

Echocardiography & Colour Doppler

High-resolution transthoracic echocardiography, strain imaging, and paediatric cardiac evaluations for structural heart diseases and valve disorders.

Stress Testing (TMT) & Holter Monitoring

Treadmill stress testing (TMT) for occult coronary artery disease and 24/48-hour continuous Holter ECG recording for unexplained palpitations or syncope.

Heart Failure & Hypertension Clinic

Structured medical management, lifestyle optimization, and neurohormonal modulation for resistant hypertension and chronic congestive heart failure.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About Interventional Cardiology

Clear, evidence-based answers to help you make informed decisions about your health and treatment options.

Ask a Doctor
What is Primary Angioplasty (PAMI) and why is time critical?
Primary Angioplasty is the immediate mechanical reopening of a blocked coronary artery with a balloon and stent during an ongoing heart attack. It is the gold standard of care. Restoring blood flow within the 'Golden Hour' (door-to-balloon time < 60 mins) prevents permanent heart muscle necrosis and drastically reduces mortality.
What are the advantages of Radial Angiography (through the wrist)?
Radial access through the wrist is safer and far more comfortable than traditional femoral (groin) access. It carries significantly lower bleeding risk, allows the patient to sit up and walk immediately after the procedure, and enables same-day discharge in diagnostic cases.
What warning signs indicate I should rush to the Sukhda Emergency?
Immediate emergency evaluation is needed if you experience central chest pain, pressure or tightness radiating to the left arm, shoulder, jaw, or back, sudden shortness of breath, unexplained cold sweating, dizziness, sudden palpitations, or fainting spells.
Does Sukhda Hospital have 24/7 emergency cardiac facilities?
Yes. Sukhda Multispeciality Hospital operates a 24/7 dedicated Cardiac Emergency Unit with an on-call interventional cardiology team, ready Cath Lab, and intensive Cardiac Care Unit (CCU) available round-the-clock.
Are angioplasty and cardiac procedures covered under cashless insurance?
Yes. Sukhda Hospital is empanelled with major private health insurers, TPAs, Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY), CGHS, ECHS, and Haryana Government schemes for cashless cardiac treatments and stenting.
