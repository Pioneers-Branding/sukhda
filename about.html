ABOUT SUKHDA MULTISPECIALTY HOSPITAL

Care & Cure for the Whole Family under One Roof

Sukhda Multispeciality Hospital is the brainchild of Dr. Amit Mehta, MD (Medicine), AIIMS, New Delhi. After completing his education and gaining valuable clinical experience at premier institutions such as AIIMS, New Delhi, and PGIMER, Chandigarh, Dr. Mehta returned to Hisar with the vision of providing quality healthcare to the community.

In November 2002, Sukhda Hospital was established with the mission of "Care & Cure for the Whole Family under One Roof." Over the years, the hospital has grown into a 125-bedded NABH Accredited Multispecialty Hospital, offering comprehensive medical, surgical, diagnostic, and emergency healthcare services.

Equipped with advanced medical technology, experienced specialists, and modern infrastructure, the hospital continues to provide quality healthcare at an affordable cost. In 2022, the hospital was accredited by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) to conduct the prestigious DNB General Medicine Programme.

Today, Sukhda Healthcare operates as a unified healthcare ecosystem comprising Sukhda Multispeciality Hospital and Sukhda Med Park (Cancer & Super Speciality Hospital), delivering ethical and evidence-based patient care to families across Haryana, Punjab, and Rajasthan.

HISTORICAL MILESTONES

Our Growth Timeline (2002 – 2025)

From a 20-bed hospital in 2002 to a 125-bed NABH Accredited multispeciality and comprehensive cancer care institution.

2002

The Beginning

Sukhda Hospital was established as a 20-bedded hospital with two in-house specialists in the Departments of General Medicine and Radiology, with a vision to develop a comprehensive multispecialty healthcare facility.

20 Beds General Medicine Radiology
2006

Expansion of Clinical Services

The Departments of General & Laparoscopic Surgery and Obstetrics & Gynaecology were introduced with full-time specialists, increasing the hospital capacity to 45 beds.

45 Beds Laparoscopic Surgery Obstetrics & Gynaecology
2007

Critical Care Services

The Critical Care Department was established with a 6-bedded Intensive Care Unit (ICU), and the hospital's total bed capacity was expanded to 60 beds.

60 Beds 6-Bed Dedicated ICU Critical Care Protocols
2012

Infrastructure Expansion

Hospital infrastructure was significantly enhanced, increasing the total bed capacity to 100 beds to meet the rapidly growing healthcare needs of Western Haryana and neighbouring regions.

100 Beds Advanced Diagnostics Regional Healthcare Hub
2015

New Specialties & ECHS Empanelment

Sukhda Hospital received ECHS Empanelment, enabling healthcare services for Ex-Servicemen and their dependents. The Departments of Urology, Neurosurgery, and Orthopaedics were also introduced.

ECHS Empanelled Neurosurgery Urology Orthopaedics
2016

Comprehensive Multispecialty Growth

The hospital expanded its sanctioned capacity to 125 beds and added new specialties, including Dermatology, ENT, and Psychiatry, further strengthening its multidisciplinary healthcare services.

125 Sanctioned Beds ENT Dermatology Psychiatry
2020

National Recognition for Quality

Sukhda Hospital achieved Full NABH Accreditation and was awarded the AB PM-JAY Gold Quality Certification, reflecting its commitment to quality healthcare, patient safety, and clinical excellence.

Full NABH Accreditation AB PM-JAY Gold Quality Patient Safety Benchmarks
2022

Clinical Excellence & Academic Milestones

The hospital continued its rapid growth through landmark clinical and academic milestones:
• Launching the Gastroenterology Department with advanced Endoscopy, ERCP, and related diagnostic services.
• Got Accredited with National Board of Examinations – Medical Sciences (NBEMS) New Delhi for DNB – General Medicine with two seats.
• Partnering with the USAID Samridh & Pahel Initiative to extend primary and secondary healthcare services to eight rural areas, benefiting approximately 65,000 people.
• Introducing a Hormone Analyser in the Pathology Laboratory.
• Launching 3D Technology Knee Resurfacing in the Department of Orthopaedics.

DNB General Medicine (2 Seats) Gastroenterology & ERCP USAID Samridh (65,000 Rural Lives) 3D Knee Resurfacing
2025

Comprehensive Cancer Care

The hospital established its Medical & Surgical Oncology Department and launched a fully equipped Chemotherapy Ward featuring a Bio-Safety Cabinet (Thermo Scientific™ 1500 Series Class II Type A2) and advanced life-support equipment, expanding its comprehensive cancer care services across the region.

ADVANCED INTERVENTIONAL CARDIOLOGY

Cath Lab Specifications & Cardiac Equipment

Fully digital ARTS ZEE PURE Catheterization Lab with Flat Panel detector and advanced software (CLEAR STENT LIVE & ROADMAP). First and only Cath lab in Hisar with CLEAR STENT LIVE technology for real-time verification of stent positioning, minimizing restenosis with lowest radiation dosage.

IVUS Machine

Intravascular Ultrasound

Medical imaging device using an ultrasound probe inside coronary arteries to deliver real-time, high-resolution cross-sectional arterial images.

  • Examines inside coronary arteries in microscopic detail.
  • Measures exact diameter and size of the artery.
  • Detects plaque severity and blockage composition.
  • Ensures precise stent sizing and optimal wall apposition.
  • Reduces PCI complications and boosts angioplasty success.

FFR Machine

Fractional Flow Reserve

Diagnostic system measuring blood pressure gradient across blockages using a precision pressure guidewire to assess hemodynamic significance.

  • Measures functional impact of stenosis on myocardial blood flow.
  • Determines if narrowing requires stenting or medical therapy.
  • Prevents unnecessary stent implantation for non-critical blocks.
  • Used alongside IVUS during complex PCI procedures.
  • Optimizes long-term coronary intervention outcomes.

ACT Machine

Activated Clotting Time

Real-time point-of-care coagulometer measuring blood clotting time within minutes after heparin administration during cardiac procedures.

  • Monitors heparin anticoagulation during angiography and PCI.
  • Ensures exact blood thinning to prevent intra-catheter thrombus.
  • Calculates precise supplemental heparin dosing.
  • Prevents excessive bleeding risk from over-anticoagulation.
  • Essential for patient safety during high-risk interventions.

IABP Machine

Intra-Aortic Balloon Pump

Mechanical circulatory support device inflating and deflating synchronously with heartbeat to assist failing ventricles and stabilize cardiogenic shock.

  • Provides vital hemodynamic support in severe heart failure.
  • Reduces left ventricular afterload and myocardial oxygen demand.
  • Increases coronary perfusion and systemic cardiac output.
  • Critical support bridge during high-risk PCI and post-cardiac arrest.
  • Stabilizes patients awaiting definitive advanced therapies.

Cardiac Monitors & Defibrillators

Real-Time Vital Monitoring & Pacing

Continuous multi-parameter monitoring and biphasic defibrillation units for instant arrhythmia recognition and resuscitation.

  • Real-time ECG, NIBP, SpO2, and respiratory rate tracking.
  • Instant threshold alarms for bradycardia & tachyarrhythmias.
  • Synchronized cardioversion for conscious tachycardias.
  • Biphasic defibrillation for VF/pulseless VT arrest.
  • External transcutaneous pacing for severe heart block.

ECHO & Holter ECG Recorder

2D/3D Color Doppler & 24–48 Hr ECG

Non-invasive high-resolution Echocardiography and ambulatory continuous Holter recording for silent arrhythmias and cardiac function evaluation.

  • Measures chamber dimensions, ejection fraction (EF), and valves.
  • Detects pericardial effusion and congenital cardiac defects.
  • 24 to 48-hour continuous ambulatory Holter recording.
  • Diagnoses unexplained syncope, palpitations, and dizziness.
  • Evaluates pacemaker function and antiarrhythmic efficacy.
