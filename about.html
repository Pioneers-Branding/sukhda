Two Decades of Clinical Excellence,
Compassion & Quality Care

Founded in November 2002 by Dr. Amit Mehta, MD (Medicine), AIIMS, New Delhi, Sukhda Multispeciality Hospital has grown from a 20-bed hospital to a 125-bed NABH-Accredited Multispeciality Hospital and DNB Teaching Centre, guided by the mission of "Care & Cure for the Whole Family under One Roof". We are en route to the successful completion of 25 years of clinical excellence and clinical outcomes, as every member of our clinical, administrative and support teams believes that trust is built through clinical outcomes and an organisation is developed through effective administration.

TWO DECADES OF HEALTHCARE EXCELLENCE

Our Journey & Milestones

From a 20-bedded clinic in 2002 to a 125-bedded NABH-accredited tertiary multispeciality hospital & comprehensive cancer centre.

2002

2002 – The Beginning

Sukhda Hospital was established as a 20-bedded hospital with two in-house specialists in the Departments of General Medicine and Radiology, with a vision to develop a comprehensive multispecialty healthcare facility.

20 Beds Foundation General Medicine Radiology Visionary Healthcare
2006

2006 – Expansion of Clinical Services

The Departments of General & Laparoscopic Surgery and Obstetrics & Gynaecology were introduced with full-time specialists, increasing the hospital capacity to 45 beds.

45 Beds Capacity General & Laparoscopic Surgery Obstetrics & Gynaecology Full-Time Specialists
2007

2007 – Critical Care Services

The Critical Care Department was established with a 6-bedded Intensive Care Unit (ICU), and the hospital's total bed capacity was expanded to 60 beds.

60 Beds Total 6-Bedded ICU Critical Care Department 24/7 Emergency Support
2012

2012 – Infrastructure Expansion

Hospital infrastructure was significantly enhanced, increasing the total bed capacity to 100 beds to meet the growing healthcare needs of the region.

100 Beds Milestone Advanced Infrastructure Regional Healthcare Hub
2015

2015 – New Specialties & ECHS Empanelment

Sukhda Hospital received ECHS Empanelment, enabling healthcare services for Ex-Servicemen and their dependents. The Departments of Urology, Neurosurgery, and Orthopaedics were also introduced.

ECHS Empanelment Urology Neurosurgery Orthopaedics
2016

2016 – Comprehensive Multispecialty Growth

The hospital expanded its sanctioned capacity to 125 beds and added new specialties, including Dermatology, ENT, Psychiatry, further strengthening its multidisciplinary healthcare services.

125 Beds Sanctioned Dermatology ENT Psychiatry
2020

2020 – National Recognition for Quality

Sukhda Hospital achieved Full NABH Accreditation and was awarded the AB PM-JAY Gold Quality Certification, reflecting its commitment to quality healthcare, patient safety, and clinical excellence.

Full NABH Accreditation AB PM-JAY Gold Quality Certification Patient Safety & Clinical Excellence
2022

2022 – Clinical Excellence & Academic Milestones

The hospital continued its growth by:

  • Gastroenterology & Endoscopy: Launching the Gastroenterology Department with advanced Endoscopy, ERCP, and related diagnostic services.
  • Academic Accreditation: Got Accredited with National Board of Examinations – Medical Sciences (NBEMS) New Delhi for DNB – General Medicine with two seats.
  • Rural Health Outreach: Partnering with the USAID Samridh & Pahel Initiative to extend primary and secondary healthcare services to eight rural areas, benefiting approximately 65,000 people.
  • Laboratory Advancement: Introducing a Hormone Analyser in the Pathology Laboratory.
  • Orthopaedic Innovation: Launching 3D Technology Knee Resurfacing in the Department of Orthopaedics.
Gastroenterology & ERCP DNB General Medicine USAID Samridh & Pahel Hormone Analyser 3D Knee Resurfacing
2025

2025 – Comprehensive Cancer Care

The hospital established its Medical & Surgical Oncology Department and launched a fully equipped Chemotherapy Ward featuring a Bio-Safety Cabinet and advanced life-support equipment, expanding its comprehensive cancer care services.

Medical & Surgical Oncology Bio-Safety Cabinet Chemotherapy Advanced Life-Support Comprehensive Cancer Care
