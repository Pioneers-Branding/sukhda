Why Choose Sukhda?

Empowering Your Medical & Professional Growth

We foster a culture of clinical excellence, innovation, and continuous learning where doctors, nurses, and allied professionals thrive.

Advanced Medical Technology

Work with state-of-the-art infrastructure including Philips Flat-Panel Cath Lab, Varian Linear Accelerator (LINAC), Modular Laminar OTs, 3D Arthroscopy, and advanced ICU ventilators.

DNB Academics & Research

Accredited by NBEMS for Post-Graduate DNB in General Medicine. Learn and practice alongside AIIMS-trained faculty Dr. Amit Mehta with active clinical audits, mortality rounds, and journal clubs.

NABH Quality & Safety Culture

Rigorous clinical governance led by Dr. Manisha Mehta (Certified NABH Assessor). Practice in an environment that puts patient safety, transparent protocols, and infection control first.

Competitive Rewards & Benefits

Attractive remuneration packages commensurate with qualifications and merit, Provident Fund (PF), ESI, gratuity, medical coverage for family, and performance-linked bonuses.

Nursing & Paramedical Empowerment

Structured onboarding, BLS/ACLS certifications, simulation-based workshops, infection control up-skilling, and clear clinical leadership progression pathways.

Inclusive, Supportive Community

Collaborative multidisciplinary teamwork across departments, on-campus accommodation assistance for outstation candidates, and active welfare committees.

Academic Excellence

NBEMS Accredited DNB Residency Program

Sukhda Multispeciality Hospital is accredited by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) for Post-Graduate DNB (General Medicine) residency training. Our academic curriculum blends intensive bedside clinical exposure, ICU rotations, research, and thesis guidance under AIIMS New Delhi alumni.
