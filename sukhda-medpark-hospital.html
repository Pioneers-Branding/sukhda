360-DEGREE ONCOLOGY SPECTRUM

Comprehensive Cancer Care Modalities

State-of-the-art medical, surgical, and radiation modalities engineered to maximize cure rates and quality of life.

Medical Oncology & Day Care Suites

Equipped with ergonomic chemotherapy infusion recliners, infusion pumps, and certified oncology pharmacists preparing therapies under sterile Bio-Safety hoods.

  • Systemic Chemotherapy: Adjuvant, neo-adjuvant & palliative regimens.
  • Immunotherapy & Checkpoint Inhibitors: Enhancing the body’s immune system to destroy malignant cells.
  • Targeted Therapy & Precision Oncology: Monoclonal antibodies and tyrosine kinase inhibitors tailored to genetic mutations.
  • Hormonal Therapy: For breast, prostate, and neuroendocrine malignancies.

Surgical Oncology & Modular OTs

High-precision surgical oncology performed in HEPA-filtered, laminar airflow modular operating suites with direct ICU backup.

  • Head & Neck Onco-Surgery: Oral cavity, thyroid, parotid, and reconstructive microvascular surgeries.
  • Breast Onco-Surgery: Breast Conservation Surgeries (BCS), Sentinel Lymph Node Biopsy, and modified radical mastectomies.
  • Gastrointestinal & Hepatobiliary Oncology: Esophageal, stomach, colorectal, liver, and pancreatic resections.
  • Gynaecological Onco-Surgery: Ovarian, uterine, and cervical oncological procedures.
  • Uro-Oncology & Thoracic Surgery: Kidney, bladder, prostate, and lung cancer surgeries.

Radiation Oncology & LINAC

High-energy Linear Accelerator (LINAC) facility delivering pinpoint ionizing radiation to destroy tumor cells while sparing adjacent healthy tissue.

  • 3D-CRT (3D Conformal Radiation Therapy): Shaped precisely to the three-dimensional tumor volume.
  • IMRT / IGRT: Intensity-Modulated and Image-Guided Radiation Therapy.
  • CT Simulator: High-precision simulation and dosimetry planning for exact beam alignment.
  • Brachytherapy: High Dose Rate (HDR) internal radiation for localized targets.
