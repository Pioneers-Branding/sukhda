Patient Information

Guidelines for OPD Patients

Everything you need to know before visiting our OPD consultation clinics.

Registration & Queue

OPD registration opens daily from 08:30 AM at the central reception desk. Appointments booked online receive priority slot tokens.

  • Token numbers are called in sequential order
  • Follow-up visits valid within 7 days
  • Emergency patients fast-tracked to Casualty

What to Bring Along

Carrying your complete medical history allows our specialists to provide accurate evaluation and avoid repeated diagnostics.

  • Government Photo ID Proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID)
  • Past prescriptions, discharge summaries & X-rays/CT scans
  • Ayushman Card / Mediclaim TPA Card & Policy Details

24/7 Emergency & Admission

Sukhda Healthcare provides round-the-clock emergency medical services, trauma surgery, CCU/ICU, and emergency dialysis.

  • 24/7 ER Helpline: 01662-249473 / 248473 / 99966-48483
  • WhatsApp Coordinator: +91 99965-44005
  • Dedicated Cashless TPA counter open 24/7

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about booking, OPD slots, consultation timings, and emergency access.

You can book online by clicking the "Book Appointment" button on this page, calling our dedicated OPD helpline at 01662-249473 / 248473 / 99966-48483, or sending a message on WhatsApp at +91 99965-44005. Walk-in registrations are also available at the hospital reception.
Most senior consultants hold morning clinics from 10:30 AM to 03:00 PM and evening clinics from 05:00 PM to 07:30 PM. Specific timings and days for each doctor are listed in the schedule table above.
Routine OPD consultations are covered under select comprehensive health policies (OPD cover / Corporate tie-ups). For daycare procedures, chemotherapy, dialysis, and emergency admissions, 24/7 cashless hospitalization is available across 69+ empanelled TPAs and insurance companies.
Sukhda Hospital operates a full-service 24/7 Emergency & Trauma Centre with resident medical officers, emergency physicians, ICU intensivist coverage, emergency lab, CT scan, and on-call senior specialists 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
Please call our reception desk at 01662-249473 / 248473 / 99966-48483 or message us on WhatsApp at +91 99965-44005 at least 2 hours prior to your slot, and our team will gladly reschedule your visit.
