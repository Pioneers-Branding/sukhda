ABOUT SUKHDA HEALTHCARE

Over Two Decades of Excellence,
Compassion & Quality Care

Sukhda Multispeciality Hospital is the brainchild of Dr. Amit Mehta, MD (Medicine), AIIMS, New Delhi. After gaining rich clinical experience at AIIMS, New Delhi and PGIMER, Chandigarh, Dr. Mehta established Sukhda in November 2002 with the mission of "Care & Cure for the Whole Family under One Roof".

Over the years, Sukhda has expanded into a 125-bed NABH-Accredited Multispeciality Hospital and an NBEMS-accredited DNB General Medicine Teaching Centre. Under the administrative and quality leadership of Dr. Manisha Mehta (NABH-Certified Assessor), the network has grown to include Sukhda Multispeciality Hospital and Sukhda MedPark (Cancer & Super-Speciality Hospital), delivering top-tier healthcare at an affordable cost across Haryana, Punjab, and Rajasthan.
